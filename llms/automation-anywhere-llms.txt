# Automation Anywhere
 
> The #1 provider of agentic automation for the Autonomous Enterprise. Automation Anywhere provides agentic automation that empowers employees to focus on what matters most, orchestrating AI agents, bots, automations, and human decision points into unified, end-to-end business processes.
 
The Agentic Process Automation (APA) platform orchestrates the world's instruments of work, AI agents, bots, automations, and human decision points into unified, end-to-end processes across any system, fully platform-agnostic. Powered by metadata from 450M+ executions across every industry and use case, APA applies proven patterns with enterprise-grade governance and observability across every agent, step, and decision. The result: an expanded automation frontier, lower-cost execution through right-fit tool choice, and higher resilience through smarter escalations, compounding into end-to-end business outcomes.

Automation Anywhere is recognized as a Leader in the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Robotic Process Automation for the eighth year in a row, and is committed to responsible, human-centered AI, with ISO 27001, ISO 9001, ISO 42001, ISO 22301, SOC 1, SOC 2, HIPAA, and HITRUST certifications, enabling organizations to innovate confidently without compromising control or compliance.
