This guide explores how agentic process automation transforms HR from a manual coordination function into an autonomous execution layer, addressing the "Coordination Tax" where 60% of HR time is spent managing handoffs between disconnected platforms like Workday and ServiceNow. The article covers applications spanning talent acquisition, employee onboarding, service delivery, and talent management, while addressing ethical concerns including algorithmic bias, data privacy, and the importance of human oversight in high-stakes decisions.
